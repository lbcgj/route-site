Purpose-Built Tools for Local Church Ministries

Every feature in ROUTE has been crafted alongside active church bus captains, soulwinning directors, and ministry leaders.

Request Access

1. Bus & Van Route Operations

Manage every aspect of your Sunday morning, Wednesday night, and special event bus routes. Keep track of every child, teenager, and adult rider with real-time status updates.

  • Dynamic Stop Ordering: Drag-and-drop or auto-optimize pickup order for fastest transit times.
  • Instant Attendance Lists: One-tap check-ins with photo profiles and emergency contact info.
  • Driver & Helper Assignments: Clear division of labor between drivers, captains, and assistant helpers.
  • Vehicle Maintenance Logs: Track fuel levels, oil changes, tire pressure, and inspection logs.

2. Soulwinning & Visitation Management

Turn Saturday soulwinning and weekly visitation into an organized, high-yield ministry. Never lose track of a prospect or visitor again.

  • Prospect Geolocation: Map prospect addresses and view door-to-door visitation lists.
  • Door Visit Notes: Record visit outcomes, prayer requests, and salvation decisions.
  • Soulwinning Check-In Counter: Track active visitation workers and overall neighborhood outreach numbers.
  • Orphaned Visits Routing: Re-assign unvisited prospects to available bus workers automatically.

3. RoutePhone Telephony & Messaging

A dedicated telephony engine built directly into your ministry platform. Send arrival notices to parents and keep your team connected.

  • Automated Parent SMS Alerts: Send instant notifications when the bus leaves church or is 10 minutes from a stop.
  • Internal Team Chat Room: Real-time messaging between captains, drivers, helpers, and church leadership.
  • Voice & Call Routing: Receive parent calls on official church numbers and route them to the active bus captain.

4. District & Territory Spatial Mapping

Visualize your city, establish route boundaries, and optimize coverage across every neighborhood.

  • Custom District Boundaries: Draw geographical zones to assign to specific bus captains.
  • Rider Density Heatmaps: Identify high-growth neighborhoods needing new routes or vans.
  • Turn-by-Turn Guidance: Optimized routing paths for drivers during Sunday morning pickups.
