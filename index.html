Ministry Impact

Real Solutions for Real Church Challenges

We built ROUTE to solve the daily frustrations bus directors, captains, drivers, and pastors face every week.

No More Lost Paper Lists

Stop relying on soggy, handwritten clipboard notes. Every bus captain, driver, and assistant has their full route roster right on their mobile device or tablet.

Get Home Faster Every Sunday

Automated stop ordering eliminates unnecessary driving loops, saving fuel and getting your bus workers home on time for Sunday lunch with their families.

Peace of Mind for Parents

Automatic text messages let parents know exact pickup times so children are dressed, ready, and waiting at the curb when the bus pulls up.

Follow Up on Every Soulwinning Visitor

Keep track of every door visited during Saturday soulwinning, log prayer requests, and ensure prospective families receive a warm follow-up visit before Sunday.

Clear Territory Maps for Workers

Visual map boundaries ensure soulwinning teams and bus captains know exactly which neighborhoods and streets belong to their route.

100% Private Church Data

Your church's family rosters and child attendance records belong strictly to your church. Your data is isolated, protected, and never shared.

Designed for Workers in the Field

Easy enough for any bus worker to use on a smartphone, powerful enough for church secretaries and pastors in the office.
