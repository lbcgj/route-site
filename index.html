Built For Church Ministry

Everything Your Bus & Visitation Teams Need

Designed specifically for local church bus captains, drivers, soulwinning workers, and ministry leaders.

Bus & Van Route Operations

Easily manage route stop sequences, live rider pick-up/drop-off check-ins, driver assignments, and vehicle maintenance logs in real time.

Soulwinning & Visitation

Track prospective families, log door-to-door visit notes, assign territory maps to soulwinning teams, and manage spiritual decision records.

RoutePhone Communications

Dedicated church ministry phone system with automated SMS alerts to parents ("Bus arriving in 10 mins!"), team chat room, and call logging.

District & Territory Mapping

Visual map assignments showing prospect locations, bus route boundaries, rider density heatmaps, and optimal route ordering.

Enterprise Security & RLS Isolation

Every church tenant's data is strictly isolated with Row-Level Security policies. Complete protection for rider and family privacy.

Seamless Data Import

Effortlessly migrate existing bus records, rider rosters, and prospect lists from spreadsheets or legacy files in seconds.

Designed for Fast Mobile & Desktop Operation

Whether bus captains are on tablets in the field or secretaries are in the church office, ROUTE responds instantly.

Real-Time Route Management

Keep your bus captains organized with one-tap rider check-ins, instant stop re-ordering, and driver navigation modes.

  • Rapid attendance tracking per bus stop
  • Automated notification alerts to parents
  • Driver and helper team assignments
