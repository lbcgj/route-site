Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Church Privacy

At ROUTE, we understand the sensitive nature of local church bus rosters, family contact information, child attendance data, and soulwinning visitation logs. We are committed to protecting your church's data with strict technical safeguards.

2. Information We Collect

When using ROUTE (via app.routeapp.org or preview request forms on routeapp.org), we may process:

  • Church & Account Details: Church name, pastor/director contact information, email address, and vehicle fleet sizes.
  • Bus Roster & Rider Data: Rider names, guardian contact details, stop locations, and Sunday attendance logs entered by your bus captains.
  • Soulwinning & Prospect Data: Prospect addresses, door-to-door visit records, prayer requests, and soulwinning decision check-ins.
  • Telephony & SMS Logs: Call duration, timestamp, recipient phone numbers, and message status for RoutePhone notifications.

3. Database Row-Level Security (RLS) & Data Isolation

ROUTE utilizes PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies across all database models. Data is strictly scoped to your specific church tenant context:

  • No tenant can view, query, or modify another church organization's records.
  • Central database sessions automatically enforce tenant boundary resets.
  • Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard protocols.

4. How We Use Information

Ministry data collected within ROUTE is used exclusively to:

  • Power your bus route stop sequences, driver navigation, and rider attendance tracking.
  • Dispatch automated SMS arrival notices to guardians ("Bus is 10 mins away").
  • Organize Saturday soulwinning maps and prospect follow-ups for your ministry team.

We NEVER sell, trade, or monetize your church's roster or contact data to third parties.

5. Telephony & Mobile SMS Privacy

RoutePhone SMS capabilities are strictly reserved for ministry communication. Mobile phone numbers collected for guardian alerts are never shared with external advertisers or un-affiliated third parties.

6. Preview Access Program Data Policy

Information submitted on preview request forms is used solely to evaluate your church's onboarding readiness for our preview program prior to our official public launch in January 2028.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Churches may export their complete roster data, visit logs, and attendance records at any time. Upon request, a tenant organization's data can be purged permanently from our database servers.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, security architecture, or data isolation, please reach out via our Contact Page or log in at app.routeapp.org.
