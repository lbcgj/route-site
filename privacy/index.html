Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Data Controller & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how David J. Spillman operating the ROUTE Platform ("ROUTE", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information obtained through our website at routeapp.org and our application software portal at app.routeapp.org.

ROUTE is a specialized multi-tenant operating system designed for local church bus ministries, soulwinning visitation, and route logistics. We understand that church rosters, child attendance records, emergency numbers, and soulwinning visitation notes represent deeply sensitive personal and ministry data. We maintain strict technical, legal, and operational safeguards to protect this information.

2. Zero Data Selling & Monetization Guarantee

We hold an absolute commitment to data privacy. ROUTE never sells, rents, trades, licenses, or monetizes any church data, rider information, phone numbers, or soulwinning notes to any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

All information collected within ROUTE is used strictly to power your church's bus route operations, parent text updates, worker coordination, and outreach tracking.

3. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

Depending on your interaction with ROUTE, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Church Account & Worker Profile Information: Church name, pastor/director names, staff contact emails, assigned bus route roles (bus captains, drivers, secretaries, helpers), and encrypted account credentials.
  • Bus Roster & Rider PII (Including Minors): Rider names, ages/birth dates, home pickup/drop-off physical addresses, parent/guardian names, emergency telephone numbers, medical/care notes (e.g., allergies or minor injury incident notes), and weekly bus attendance logs.
  • Soulwinning & Outreach Notes: Prospect names, home addresses, visit check-in timestamps, prayer requests, and spiritual decision records entered by authorized soulwinning teams.
  • Telephony & Communication Data: Mobile phone numbers, opt-in consent records, outbound text message timestamps, message delivery statuses, and worker-initiated SMS content.
  • Technical & Operational Logs: Secure access timestamps, IP addresses, and encrypted session tokens required to authenticate user sessions and maintain system security.

4. Telephony & Twilio SMS Messaging Compliance

ROUTE integrates with telephony providers, including Twilio Inc., to enable direct communications between church workers and parents or ministry team members.

A. Nature of Messaging:
All text messages sent through ROUTE are human-initiated and composed individually by authorized church staff or bus workers through our telephony interface. Messages are operational and transactional in nature (e.g., "We are on our way with the church bus!", "Will Joseph be riding the bus tomorrow?", or child care check-in updates).

B. Consent & Opt-In Method:
Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are obtained directly when parents/guardians register their children in person with church bus workers via signed physical permission slips or enrollment forms. Bus workers explicitly inform parents that the ministry uses ROUTE to communicate bus schedules, rider updates, and emergency notices.

C. Mandatory Non-Sharing Clause:
No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

D. Opt-Out & Support Commands:
Message recipients can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE to any text message received. For assistance, recipients can reply HELP or contact privacy@routeapp.org. Message frequency varies based on church route activity. Standard message and data rates may apply.

5. AWS Infrastructure & Email Policy

ROUTE infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) within the United States (`us-east-2` region).

A. Transactional Emails (AWS SES):
Email communications are delivered via AWS Simple Email Service (SES). We send strictly transactional and administrative emails (such as worker account onboarding, password resets, system alerts, and weekly route summaries). We do not send unsolicited marketing emails. Every administrative email contains valid sender identification, contact details, and an unsubscribe link where applicable.

B. Infrastructure Security:
All data transmitted between your device and ROUTE is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. All stored data, including database volumes and media assets, is encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

6. Multi-Tenant Architecture & Data Isolation

ROUTE enforces strict tenant data isolation. Each church organization operates within its own secured logical environment utilizing PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. No other church, tenant, or unauthorized party can ever access, query, or view your ministry’s rider rosters, phone numbers, or visitation records.

7. Children’s Privacy Safeguards (COPPA)

Because local church bus ministries serve children and youth, ROUTE processes information regarding minor riders. We strictly comply with Children's Online Privacy Protection principles:

  • Child rider data is added to ROUTE only after parents or legal guardians complete a signed permission slip or direct enrollment with church bus staff.
  • Rider information (child name, age, pickup address, emergency contact) is used exclusively for bus transportation safety, attendance verification, and parent communication.
  • Child data is never profiled, never targeted for advertising, and never disclosed to commercial entities.
  • Parents or legal guardians may review, update, or request the deletion of their child’s rider profile at any time by contacting their local church administrator or emailing privacy@routeapp.org.

8. Protection of Soulwinning & Sensitive Data

Door-to-door visitation notes, prospect addresses, prayer requests, and spiritual decisions recorded during soulwinning outreach are treated as confidential ministry assets. Access is strictly scoped to authorized church users. ROUTE employees and automated systems do not inspect or read spiritual visitation notes except when required to deliver system support at the explicit request of the church account administrator.

9. Analytics, Cookies & Tracking

Zero Advertising Trackers: We do NOT use third-party marketing cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or remarketing tags on routeapp.org or app.routeapp.org. We utilize only essential, cryptographically secure session cookies required to keep users safely logged in.

10. Third-Party Service Subprocessors

ROUTE partners with select, trusted cloud infrastructure subprocessors strictly to deliver platform functionality:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud application hosting, encrypted database storage, and AWS SES email delivery.
  • Twilio Inc.: Telephony, SMS message routing, and voice API delivery.
  • Stripe Inc.: Secure payment processing for church subscription billing (when enabled).
  • Self-Hosted Infrastructure: Internal Pelias geocoding and OSRM routing engine services hosted on isolated, encrypted private servers.

11. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion Schedule

Churches maintain full control over their ministry records:

  • Immediate Soft Deletion: When a church administrator or parent requests the deletion of a rider, worker, or visitation record, or when a church closes its account, records are immediately soft-deleted and removed from active system visibility.
  • 60-Day Purge Window: Complete, permanent erasure from encrypted backup systems occurs within a maximum of 60 days following account termination or deletion requests.

12. Your Data Rights & Contact Information

Subject to applicable laws, church administrators and parents have the right to access, inspect, correct, export, or request the permanent deletion of their personal information.
