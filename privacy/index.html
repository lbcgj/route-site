Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. Uncompromising Protection for Church Families

At ROUTE, we understand that local church bus rosters, child attendance records, and soulwinning visitation notes are deeply personal. We are committed to protecting your church's data with strict privacy safeguards.

2. Information We Process

When using ROUTE (via app.routeapp.org or application forms on routeapp.org), we process:

  • Church Account Details: Church name, pastor/director contact information, and bus fleet sizes.
  • Bus Roster & Rider Data: Rider names, guardian emergency contact numbers, stop locations, and Sunday attendance records entered by your bus workers.
  • Soulwinning & Outreach Notes: Prospect addresses, door-to-door visit notes, prayer requests, and soulwinning decision check-ins.

3. Strict Church Data Isolation

Every church organization's data is strictly isolated within our system boundaries. No other church, tenant, or outside party can ever view, query, or modify your ministry records.

4. Zero Data Selling Guarantee

Ministry data collected within ROUTE is used exclusively to power your bus routes, parent arrival text updates, and soulwinning tracking. We NEVER sell, trade, or monetize your church's family data.

5. Contact & Privacy Questions

If you have questions about our data protection practices, please contact us via our Contact Page or log in at app.routeapp.org.
